How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

  • Green — correct letter, correct place.
  • Yellow — correct letter, wrong place.
  • Gray — letter is not in the word.

Use a hint when you're stuck. Green positions are never repeated as hints.

Keep solving words to build your score. Giving up or failing resets it to zero.
